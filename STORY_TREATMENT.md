## 🎭 Major Cast & Core Mechanics

* **Leo D’Souza ("The Phantom"):** A bio-engineered hitman whose hyper-accelerated regeneration fixes severe wounds in seconds, but inflicts unbearable nerve pain and burns thousands of calories, leaving him physically drained after every engagement.
* **Meera Anand:** Beloved founder of the *New Root Foundation* for orphans; secretly the corrupt majority stakeholder in *Virelix Biogenics*.
* **Inspector Rakesh Bhandari:** A cynical, 54-year-old Mumbai police detective who uncovers the truth behind Leo's killing spree.
* **Priya (Subject 14):** A 12-year-old runaway subject whose neural dampening abilities temporarily dull pain and cellular signals.

---

## ACT I: THE IGNITION

### Section 1 — The Biological Burden
The narrative opens in the rainy underbelly of Mumbai. Leo D’Souza carries out a night raid on a high-security lab belonging to *Virelix Biogenics*. Equipped with custom ring-pivoting Karambit gauntlets, Leo moves with terrifying efficiency. During the assault, he takes a point-blank shotgun blast to the chest. 

Instead of dying, his biological curse activates: the hyper-accelerated cellular regeneration forces muscles and bone to stitch back together within seconds. The process is not a smooth superpower—it is violent, excruciating metabolic torture. Bones snap violently into place, skin burns, and Leo collapsed in agony, severely calorie-depleted before forcing himself to finish the job and burn the facility to the ground.

### Section 2 — The Saint and the Detective
Leo returns to a secluded sanctuary managed by Meera Anand, founder of the *New Root Foundation*. Meera nurtured Leo after he survived a catastrophic laboratory explosion at age five. She treats his wounds, feeds him high-calorie supplements, and reinforces his belief that his kills are targeted strikes against evil corporate figures trying to harm her orphanages.

Simultaneously, Inspector Rakesh Bhandari arrives at the charred *Virelix* crime scene. While his corrupt superiors dismiss the break-in as routine gang violence, Bhandari’s sharp detective instincts notice inconsistencies: blood splatters indicating a fatal chest wound, but no corpse, along with clean, surgical cuts on security guards. Dismissed by his precinct as an aging, washed-up officer, Bhandari quietly opens a private case file.

### Section 3 — The Catalyst
Leo receives his next target: Dr. Ananya Sen, a chief bio-researcher at *Virelix*. Leo ambushes her vehicle in an isolated parking garage. Before he can strike, Dr. Sen panics and reveals she isn't a villain—she is a whistleblower attempting to leak evidence that *Virelix* tests bio-agents on missing street children. 

Before Leo can process this, a stealth tactical squad sent by *Virelix* opens fire, executing Dr. Sen. In the crossfire, Leo discovers a frightened 12-year-old girl hidden in the trunk: Priya (Subject 14).

---

## ACT II: THE DISCOVERY & THE PURGE

### Section 1 — The Revelation
Leo escapes with Priya to a safehouse. When his wounds flare up, Priya touches his arm; her unique biological ability dampens cellular signals, instantly suppressing Leo's overwhelming pain. 

Priya reveals the truth: she escaped from a hidden subterranean wing of the *New Root Foundation*. She hands Leo encrypted digital files saved by Dr. Sen. The financial documents expose that Meera Anand is not protecting orphans—she is secretly the majority owner of *Virelix Biogenics*. She uses her orphanages as a pipeline for human subjects and deployed Leo as her unwitting hitman to execute corporate whistleblowers and rivals.

### Section 2 — The Shift to the Purge
Realizing his entire life and moral crusade were engineered by the woman he trusted as a mother, Leo's purpose fractures. He abandons surgical strikes and commits to a full, systematic purge of Meera’s syndicate. 

Leo launches brutal, high-impact assaults against *Virelix* supply depots, illegal testing clinics, and shell-company headquarters across Mumbai. His combat style becomes increasingly relentless, trading his own physical safety to tear through armed security forces, relying on raw endurance and his Karambit gauntlets.

### Section 3 — Bhandari Closes In
Inspector Bhandari tracks the trail of destruction left by Leo's purge. Reconstructing crime scenes using forensic trajectory analysis, Bhandari deduces that the killer possesses abnormal physical recovery capabilities. 

Bhandari tracks down the financial link connecting the dead executives to *Virelix* and *New Root Foundation*. His corrupt superiors order him to drop the investigation, but a public media leak labels Bhandari a "sold-out cop," reigniting his pride. He resolves to bring down both the vigilante and the corporate syndicate.

---

## ACT III: CLIMAX & RESOLUTION

### Section 1 — The Siege of New Root
Leo sends Priya to Bhandari’s police precinct with a drive containing the complete financial evidence, ensuring the truth will reach the public. Leo then infiltrates the heavily fortified *New Root Foundation* compound during an elite corporate gala hosted by Meera.

Leo fights through waves of augmented mercenary security. As he reaches the inner sanctum, his body suffers severe cellular fatigue from repeated regenerations. Meera confronts him, offering no apology; she views her subjects as necessary sacrifices for human evolution. She unleashes her personal chief of security—a heavily armored combatant who uses high-voltage shock weapons specifically designed to disrupt Leo's neural healing.

### Section 2 — The Final Confrontation
Weakened and on the verge of physical collapse, Leo is nearly overwhelmed. Bhandari arrives at the compound with a SWAT unit, defying orders from his precinct. Instead of arresting Leo, Bhandari recognizes that Leo is holding back the security forces while evidence downloads to the police servers. 

Bhandari provides sniper cover, taking down the chief of security. Leo uses his final reserve of strength to breach Meera’s panic room. Refusing to let her spin another media lie, Leo destroys the central servers controlling the biogenetic research and corners Meera. As police sirens flood the estate, Meera is taken into custody under full media exposure, her public reputation shattered forever.

### Section 3 — Aftermath
Meera and her corporate co-conspirators are indicted as the *Virelix* scandal hits international news. Inspector Bhandari is fully reinstated and head of a specialized task force cleaning up corporate crime. 

Leo slips away into the night before police can secure him. The final scene shows Priya safe in a protected state home, receiving an anonymous package containing a carved wooden pendant—Leo's silent promise that he is watching over her from the shadows.
