## 1. Leo D’Souza / "The Phantom" (Protagonist)

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age / Gender** | 29 / Male |
| **Role** | Protagonist / Unwitting Corporate Hitman |
| **Archetype** | Reluctant Anti-Hero / Tragic Weapon |
| **Primary Weaponry** | Custom High-Carbon Gauntlets with Ring-Pivoting Karambit Blades |

### Psychological Profile & Arc
Leo is driven by a deep psychological need for purpose and quietude. Traumatized by the lab explosion that killed his father at age five, he suffers from severe survivor's guilt and sensory sensory overload caused by his mutation. His tragic flaw is his absolute, unquestioning loyalty to Meera Anand—the only person who offered him maternal warmth in a cold world. 

When he discovers Meera used him to murder whistleblowers, his hero complex shatters into cold, methodical vengeance. His journey moves from seeking a "cure" to accepting his role as an extinguisher of corporate evil.

### Biological & Mechanical Combat Mechanics
* **Hyper-Accelerated Regeneration:** Leo’s cells multiply at 100x the human norm. Major trauma (bullet wounds, broken bones, slashed arteries) knits back together within seconds.
* **The Metabolic Curse:** Healing offers zero numbness. Muscle fibers snap like cables, bones grind violently as they reset, and skin scorches. A major regeneration event consumes 5,000+ calories in minutes, leaving his stomach ravenous and his body near physical collapse.
* **Karambit Gauntlets:** Custom forelimb gauntlets engineered for low-noise, high-speed close-quarters combat (CQC). The ring-pivoting mechanics allow instantaneous switches between open-palm grappling and reverse-blade slashing.

## 2. Inspector Rakesh Bhandari (Tritagonist)

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age / Gender** | 54 / Male |
| **Role** | Lead Investigating Officer / Systemic Foil |
| **Archetype** | Cynical Hardboiled Detective / Redeemed Bureaucrat |
| **Primary Weaponry** | Standard Issue .38 Special Revolver / Mind for Forensic Deduction |

### Psychological Profile & Arc
Bhandari is a tired, overweight cop who has spent fifteen years playing dead inside a corrupt department to secure his pension. He uses a façade of laziness, gutka-chewing indifference, and bureaucratic compliance to hide a sharp, analytical mind. 

His turning point comes when a young journalist humiliates him on national television, labeling him a "sold-out cop." Driven by wounded pride rather than heroic ideals, Bhandari uncovers the financial paper trail connecting the corporate murders. He evolves from a passive paperwork-pusher into a relentless detective who defies his own superiors to enforce real justice.

### Key Skills & Visual Markers
* **Sherlockian Forensic Deduction:** Reads crime scenes instantly—noticing minute details like dust displacement, wire-scratch marks on door latches, and body-weight leverage.
* **Systemic Mastery:** Knows every loophole, corrupt shortcut, and political pressure point within the police department, using that knowledge to operate off the grid.

## 3. Meera Anand (Antagonist)

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age / Gender** | 52 / Female |
| **Role** | Mastermind / Founder of *New Root Foundation* |
| **Archetype** | Sociopathic Manipulator / Wolf in Sheep's Clothing |
| **Primary Influence** | Public Media, Political Ties, Black-Budget Capital |

### Psychological Profile & Arc
To the public, press, and government, Meera is a living saint—dressed in crisp sarees, running orphanages, and funding free hospitals. Privately, she is the cold, calculating majority owner of *Virelix Biogenics*. She views human beings purely as genetic raw material and evolutionary steps.

Meera does not view herself as evil; she considers herself a visionary bringing about the next stage of human evolution. Her masterwork was manipulating Leo's orphan trauma to turn him into an unpaid, perfectly loyal assassin who destroyed her corporate whistleblowers under the guise of "doing good."

### Key Dynamics
* **Mother vs. Monster:** Speaks to Leo with soft, maternal tenderness even while ordering him to commit brutal executions. Her control over him is entirely psychological.

## 4. Priya / "Subject 14" (Key Ally)

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age / Gender** | 12 / Female |
| **Role** | Whistleblower / Catalyst for Truth |
| **Archetype** | Innocent Survivor / Biological Anchor |
| **Primary Ability** | Neural Signal & Cellular Dampening |

### Psychological Profile & Arc
A quiet, highly observant child who escaped the secret subterranean wing of the *New Root Foundation*. Despite her age, she possesses profound emotional maturity born from living in laboratory isolation. She serves as the moral compass of the story and the bridge between Leo and Inspector Bhandari.

### Biological Ability
* **Cellular Signal Dampening:** Priya’s nervous system emits a localized field that dulls pain and stabilizes rapid metabolic activity. When she touches Leo, his agonizing regeneration process instantly becomes painless, making her the only person who can grant him physical peace.
